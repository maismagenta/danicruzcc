On leadership

How I lead.

I.
Strategy backed by data
Opinion is a starting point, not a destination. I prioritize by business impact — mapped, measured, negotiated. The work I'm proudest of is the work I can defend with numbers.
II.
People grow disciplines, not the other way around
Disciplines scale because individual designers grow into them. I invest in individual development — building career paths, mentoring towards autonomy, and creating the tools and space for people to own their growth.
III.
Tools are leverage, including AI
I bring AI into team workflows when it removes friction, not when it removes craft. The same principle applies to processes, systems and rituals: keep what serves the work, prune what doesn't.
