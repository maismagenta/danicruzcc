Case 02
Mercado Pago · 2024–Present
UX Design Lead, team of 10

Leading design in practice.

The situation
A team of 10, stretched thin and always answering as if it were due yesterday. Feedback and decisions moved fast, but rarely got room to be rethought from a design-solution standpoint.
The outcome
A team that reclaimed its own capacity — closing with a reliable 20% buffer for the unplanned work that always shows up — used to redesign one of the product's most visible surfaces, and to rethink how work enters the team in the first place.
Mercado Pago Home — after the redesign

Leading a team of 10 designers across UX, UI and Content — from a transversal role spanning the whole product, not a single squad. Most of what changed here was project and people leadership: reorganizing how the team allocated time and ownership so decisions could be made deliberately instead of reactively. That groundwork is what made the Home redesign — and everything below — possible within a year.

Home redesign
Led the complete redesign of the Home experience — one of the product's most visible, high-stakes surfaces.
Dark mode
Instituted dark mode across the product, from design tokens through rollout.
Redesigning Pendings, with AI
Redesigned Pendings — the surface where cross-team requests land on the Home — and became one of the first teams to bring AI into validating those requests, replacing manual sign-off with a faster, auditable process.
Content rituals
Created recurring content rituals for the team — critique, calibration and knowledge-sharing built into the workflow.
Redistributing the team
Restructured how designers were allocated and how ownership was split across the process, so requests moved predictably for both sides — clear deadlines instead of ad hoc asks — cutting the "extra miles" that used to eat the team's buffer.
AI in practice
Drove the adoption of AI into the team's processes and deliverables — working hands-on with Claude, Cursor, and other tools.
0
buffer capacity reclaimed for unplanned, internal work — by the time I left

The redesign itself took under six months of hands-on design work — the rest of the year went into building the conditions for it. Happy to walk through the full story live.

Case 03
Itaú · 2020–2021
UX Writer · Onboarding redesign

Making consortium make sense.

The redesigned onboarding, cycling through its 3 screens
The challenge
Consortium is a complex financial product in Brazil — easy to misunderstand. The onboarding was packed with jargon that didn't belong in a first contact.
The approach
Listened to customer-service calls. Ran semantic research. Aligned with marketing and sales. Co-designed with the squad. Rewrote content to lead with benefit, not mechanics.
From 0% → 0% user comprehension in testing — same product, redesigned content.
